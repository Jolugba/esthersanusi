Drop your real app logos here, then reference them in js/apps.js via the "icon" field.

Recommended: square PNG or SVG, at least 120x120px, transparent or solid background.
Example names (match the icon paths in apps.js):
  fairmoney.png  courtai.png  elite.png  moj.png  cjrp.png
  stampseal.png  justease.png  healthfit.png  tyia.png
  deposit-tracker.png  esthefi.png  learning-track.png

In apps.js, set:  icon: "assets/apps/fairmoney.png"
Until you do, the app shows a clean SVG glyph instead.
