About

About Esther

Esther Sanusi (formerly Esther Tinuade Jolugba) is a Senior Software Engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria. Over 6+ years she has shipped 20+ live applications reaching more than 10 million people across Android, iOS, Windows, and macOS — in fintech, legal tech, and civic tech.

She builds with Kotlin, Kotlin Multiplatform, Jetpack Compose, and Flutter, and is now extending her craft into data science and AI to build genuinely smarter products. She grew from an Android engineer into a software engineer by shipping whatever the product needed.

Beyond engineering, Esther is the founder of the Gritty Widows Foundation, which empowers low-income widowed families, and a relentless researcher and reader who is always widening the craft.
